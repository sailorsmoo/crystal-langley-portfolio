Sommsation

A luxury wine brand built from zero, then the systems that make it run.

VP Design and Creative2020 to 202601 / 05

Use Case

Sommsation is a luxury DTC wine platform, supporting independent producers from Napa Valley to New Zealand. I joined as the VP of Design and Creative and spent six years building the brand, the digital product and managing the marketing systems to support it.

I owned the design system, the partner materials and the event presence. That work came to 75+ design assets, 25+ partner decks and 15+ event activations, plus the campaign photography and video direction behind them.
