The Challenge

From blank page to operating brand.

Sommsation needed everything at once: a brand identity that could compete in luxury wine, a marketplace that could sell it, a marketing engine that could sustain it, and an event presence that could elevate it. I built all of it, then built the systems that keep it consistent across every touchpoint, every campaign, and every new partnership.

01 — Brand Identity

A luxury wine brand built from scratch.

I created the complete visual identity for Sommsation: the wordmark, color system, typography hierarchy, photography direction, icon design, and brand voice. Every decision positioned Sommsation as the intersection of expertise and accessibility in wine. Sophisticated enough for sommeliers. Warm enough for everyone else.

Visual Identity System
Wordmark, 6-color palette (near-black, warm cream, gold accent), editorial serif + sans pairing, and photography direction with consistent golden-hour lighting.
Brand Voice
Warm, knowledgeable, slightly irreverent. Four pillars: Sophisticated, Enlightening, Modern & Traditional, Engaging. Applied across website, email, events, and partnerships.
Interactive Brand Framework
An interactive HTML brand-audit tool using the ACE+ICE scoring framework. Living documentation that scales across the portfolio, not a static PDF.
Brand Collateral System
Lookbooks, product guides, business cards, event signage, wine journals, gift cards, tri-folds, one-sheets. All templated for scale.
02 — Website & E-Commerce

A marketplace that sells like a magazine.

I designed Sommsation.com as a multi-product ecosystem: wine marketplace, subscription club, virtual tastings, concierge services, and gifting. The split-layout homepage functions as both a brand story and a utility hub. Smart features like interactive wine profile visualizations and sommelier score badges translate expert knowledge into intuitive shopping.

Interactive Wine Profiles
Visual scales for Body, Taste, and Profile on every product page. Turns sommelier knowledge into an intuitive shopping tool for non-experts.
Multi-Product Ecosystem
Marketplace + Rebel Wine Club + Virtual Tastings + Concierge + Gifting + Editorial. Six product lines under one coherent design system.
Sommelier Score Badges
Wine scores from multiple rating systems shown prominently on product cards. A trust signal that drives conversion in a credibility-dependent category.
Geo-Aware Shipping
State-level shipping toggle with geo-detection ensures customers only see wines that can legally ship to them. Compliance as UX.

03 — Campaign Creative & Production

$500K+ in annual content production.

I direct Sommsation's commercial video and photography campaigns from creative brief through on-location production to campaign deployment. Annual shoots span international wine regions. The Rebellion Campaign established a brand voice confident enough to say "We rebels deserve the best" and back it up with the product to prove it.

Rebellion Campaign
A 43-page creative direction document. Established the "rebel" brand voice for the wine club and marketplace. Directed photography, copy, and cross-channel rollout.
TASTE Video Series
Conceived and produced TASTE with Sommsation, a branded content series. Managed pre-production with Stringer production company through final delivery.
International Shoots
Directed content production in Mendoza, Italy, Willamette Valley, and California. Built creative guides for production partner K3Films to hold brand consistency on location.
AI Creative Pipeline
A production pipeline integrating generative AI with defined hand-offs between generative output, human craft, and QA. Toolset selected per pipeline need.
04 — Experiential Events

15+ premium brand activations.

From Super Bowl LX to Sundance Film Festival to South Beach Wine & Food Festival, I designed and directed experiential wine activations for premium brand partners. Everything from the initial pitch deck through environment design, collateral, on-site execution, and post-event reporting.

05 — Marketing Engine

Always-on email driving measurable revenue.

I built and operate Sommsation's Klaviyo email program from scratch. Multiple campaign types run in overlapping windows: Winery of the Week, Virtual Tastings, Ambassador sequences, and promotions. I own creative, segmentation strategy, cadence, and campaign-level reporting with weekly performance tracking.

Multi-Segment Campaigns
Winery of the Week, Virtual Tastings, Ambassador program, Rebel Wine Club, and promotions. Each with distinct creative, cadence, and segmentation rules.
Revenue Attribution
Weekly reporting with open rates, CTOR analysis, revenue attribution, delivery health, and unsub monitoring. Data drives creative decisions.
Campaign Pairing Model
Bundled email + social packages. Each campaign deploys as a coordinated set: email, Facebook, Instagram feed, and Stories with sequenced timing.
Standardized Brief System
Every campaign runs through a templated brief: recipients, content blocks, success metrics, pre-send checklist, post-send reporting. Process as product.
06 — Partnership & Sales Decks

One template. 25+ partnerships.

I designed a modular pitch deck system that scales. One master template has been customized into 25+ partnership proposals for brands like CAA, FanDuel, Robb Report, Airbnb, and Live Nation. Each deck holds brand consistency while adapting to sector-specific storytelling.

07 — The Operating System

The thing that makes all the other things work.

This is what I actually built. A multi-phase creative, design, and brand operations system that sequences creative work from foundation through scale. Tested and implemented across sister portfolio companies at multiple growth stages. Not a one-off process. Portable infrastructure.

Multi-Phase Framework
From audit through scale. Each phase has defined inputs, deliverables, and success criteria. Tested across sister portfolio companies.
Documented Task Structure
Every task has an owner, a deliverable, and a dependency chain. Nothing runs on instinct. When I'm not in the room, the work still holds.
AI Pipeline Architecture
Generative AI as defined stages with hand-offs between generation, human craft, and production QA. Not experiments. Infrastructure.
Interactive Tooling
HTML brand frameworks, phase libraries, audit scorecards, channel playbooks. Living documentation operators use daily.
