Six years of Sommsation, in one system

I built this system over six years as VP Design and Creative: 159 component sets, 146 tokens, four modes. It was composable enough to stand up fast for sister projects and companies, and it developed in parallel with Sommsation's overall brand tone and aesthetic, the digital system and the external brand built together instead of in sequence.

Four Modes

Radius and spacing aren't fixed values, they're variables with a mode per breakpoint, so a card doesn't get restyled at each width, it resolves differently. Only desktop actually redefines the radius set; tablet inherits mobile and just resizes components. Dark is a fourth mode on the same collection, not a parallel theme.

What holds, what doesn't

  1. The system covers everything I asked of it here.

    Radius/4xl at 18px is the card. The soft shadow sits inside the elevation language I already built. Display and title are large enough to carry a headline on their own. Four semantic colors cover up, careful and down. I didn't have to invent anything to make this page work, and that's the real test of a system, not the page.

  2. The four modes are the strongest structural idea in the system, and you'd never see it in a screenshot.

    Radius and spacing are variables with a mode per breakpoint, so a card resolves at 2px on mobile and 4px on desktop without a single override, and dark is a fourth mode on the same collection rather than a parallel theme. Tablet exists to resize components, not to reshape them. This is the piece of Sommsation I'd bring into any new system.

  3. The type plate is the one place on this whole site where the specimen isn't a stand-in.

    Playfair Display and Futura PT are both loaded and licensed here, so what you're reading is the actual typeface. Because the scale is a variable collection rather than fixed styles, the plate above can honestly show two numbers per tier instead of one.

  4. No monospace here.

    Every other system on this site has a mono gap worth naming. This one doesn't need closing. A luxury wine brand shouldn't read instrumented, and captions staying in Futura Book at caption size is the right call.

  5. 159 component sets serve the web best.

    Email is the harder surface to extend into, and that work is already underway. Events get printed and collateral is decks, so two of the four surfaces above still carry the brand on two faces and a gold rule alone. The system's reach is still growing past its strongest surface.

  6. The alternating rhythm reads as a house habit here.

    A luxury brand already paces its pages this way, so pairing a label block against a specimen down the page costs nothing and fits.

  7. This system was never built to hold a metric, and I wouldn't force one into it now.

    Sommsation's job is desire, not instrumentation. Nothing across the four surfaces above is a number on a card. Forcing one in would work against every other decision in the system.
