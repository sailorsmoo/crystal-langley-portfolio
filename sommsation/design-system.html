Does it repurpose?

  1. Everything the reference needs already exists

    This is the only one of the five where the layout asks for nothing the system lacks. Radius/4xl at 18px desktop is the card. The soft shadow sits inside the existing elevation language. Display and title are large enough to carry a specimen headline. Four semantic colours cover up, careful and down. After four overviews spent naming gaps, the useful result here is that there is no gap to name.

  2. The four modes

    The strongest structural idea in the portfolio, and it is invisible in a screenshot. Radius and spacing are variables with a mode per breakpoint, so a card resolves at 2px on mobile and 4px on desktop without a single override, and dark is a fourth mode on the same collection rather than a parallel theme. Worth noting that only desktop actually redefines the radius set — tablet exists to resize components, not to reshape them. Any of the other four systems would be improved more by adopting this than by adopting the layout.

  3. The type plate, at full strength

    Also the only system here whose faces are real. Playfair Display and Futura PT are both loaded and licensed, so the specimen is the typeface rather than a stand-in — Donblas shows Jost for Brandon Grotesque, Telltale shows Cinzel for Albertus. And because the scale is a variable collection, the plate can honestly print two numbers per tier instead of one.

  4. The monospace voice

    Five systems, five times the same gap. Playfair and Futura PT, no mono, and here it is least defensible to add one: a luxury wine brand should not read instrumented. The right conclusion for this system is not to adopt a data face but to accept that this layout's captions will be set in Futura Book at caption size, and lose the effect.

  5. The library, past the first surface

    The specimens above expose the real limit, and it is not the tokens. 159 component sets serve web. Email is built where components cannot go, events are printed, and collateral is decks — three of four surfaces carry the brand on two faces and a gold rule alone. The system is excellent and its reach is one surface wide. That is the gap worth documenting, not a missing colour.

  6. The alternating rhythm

    Cheap and correct, as everywhere else — and better suited here than to any of the others, because a luxury brand already paces its pages this way. This is the one system where the rhythm is not a borrow but a house habit.

  7. The content shape

    The honest failure. The reference's model is one number and one sentence per card, and this brand's job is desire, not instrumentation: nothing in the four surfaces above is a metric, and forcing one in would work against every other decision in the system. Where Sequoia needed the model and lacked the palette, Sommsation has the palette and does not want the model.
