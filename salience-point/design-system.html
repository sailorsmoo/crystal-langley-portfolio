One Platform. Ultimate Control.

Modern point of sale for wineries. A Sommsation and Halter Ranch venture, named, branded and systemized in 2025 to sell one operations platform into a pipeline of 250 plus wineries. What follows is the system as designed: positioning, type, palette, spacing, components, and a sample of the pages built on them.

Font System

One family, two weights, nine sizes. SFT Schrifted Sans Var carries title and body alike, Demi at 605 for titles, Regular at 383 for everything else, every style at 1.8 line height.

Palette and Accent

A teal blue primary running to near black, a deep teal secondary, a gold to bronze tertiary, and a neutral scale. No semantic colors are declared. Bronze is the accent and the only call to action color; the pricing screens press it into service as the check mark too.

4pt Spacing, five Buttons

Ten spacing tokens, every one a multiple of four, with a slightly wider desktop variant of the same scale. One button shape, the pill, in five fills. That is the whole component vocabulary the file documents; cards, toggles and fields exist on the screens but not as specified components.
