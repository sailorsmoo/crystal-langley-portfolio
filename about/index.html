About

I am a UI/UX product designer, marketing and creative director. Fifteen years across gaming, technology and lifestyle. And now, AI.

I led UI and UX at Telltale Games for eight years, was promoted to Lead, then took a turn into software development, where I spent six years as VP, Design and Creative at Sommsation building a luxury wine brand from the ground up. My work integrates into AI seamlessly, and I consult for companies that need senior creative leadership without the full time hire.

When I'm not tinkering on a computer, I'm running half marathons with my dogs, baking cookies with my family, building legos on Sunday morning or boxing at the local gym.
