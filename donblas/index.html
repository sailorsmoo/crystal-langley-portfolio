Donblas

A legal AI platform that quantifies the law instead of summarizing it.

Brand and Creative Direction202605 / 05

Use Case

Donblas models legal outcomes as probability rather than prose. I took it from a stock photo homepage to a category defining brand system, proven in code.

The position came first, then the wordmark, palette, type and voice built from it. The web build replaced stock photography with owned imagery and shipped as tokens and components rather than a slide deck.
