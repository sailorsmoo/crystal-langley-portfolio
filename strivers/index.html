Strivers

Consulting Lead Designer · Creative Director2020 to 202203 / 05

Use Case

Strivers took the St. James training program outside the building. I led design as consulting lead across the mobile app, the content system and the launch.

The St. James is a 450,000 square foot sports and wellness complex outside Washington, D.C. Strivers was the training program inside it, capped by the size of the building. The content model came first. Workouts, coaches and programs had to share one structure before any screen could be trusted to scale past the first content drop.
