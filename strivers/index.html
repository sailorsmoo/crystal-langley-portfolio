The Challenge

A premium training program, capped by the size of one building.

The St. James is a 450,000-square-foot sports and wellness complex outside Washington, D.C. Strivers was the youth athletics and adult performance training program housed inside it. It worked at the scale of the building's capacity. Members took classes. Sports coaches ran programs. Everything happened on the floor, in front of a trainer.

The brand needed a way to take Strivers to people the building couldn't hold. Current members on the road. People who didn't have a membership. People in other markets entirely. I came in to build that.

01 — Composable Design System

Built once. Composed many times.

Underneath everything below is one composable design system. Tokens for type and color. Component sets for the app, the launch screens, the membership marketing, and the scheduler. The screens you'll see are compositions of it. The system is the spine.

Tokens
Custom geometric Strivers display type plus body. Strivers gold against dark moody photography. St. James navy and cream alongside. Spacing, radii, and elevation built once.
Components
Cards, forms, video player, program-detail blocks, app-store frames, membership offer cards, scheduler tiles. Each built to compose, not to ship a single screen.
Patterns
App onboarding, store marketing screen, membership offer page, in-club scheduler. Same components, different compositions per surface.
Compositions
The Strivers app. The App Store launch package. St. James membership offers across three breakpoints. The Performance Club wall graphics. One system, many products.
02 — Strivers, the mobile app

The training program, in every member's pocket.

I designed the app from login through goal setting, program selection, in-app video coaching, and program details. Custom geometric wordmark, Performance Club lockup, dark moody photography, Strivers gold as the only accent.

Onboarding asked for fitness goals as four image cards. Each workout program surfaced workout count, duration, intensity, muscle groups, and equipment up front so members could decide before they ever pressed play.

Goal-first onboarding
Four image cards: burn fat, build muscle, athletic conditioning, increase flexibility. The app meets the athlete where they are before asking anything else.
Decision-ready program cards
Every program surfaces workout count, avg duration, intensity dots, muscle-group diagrams, and equipment up front. Decide before you press play.
In-app video coaching
Full-screen video with timeline scrub, paused-state preview, and framing that mirrors what a member sees in-person on the gym floor.
A wordmark built for type
Custom geometric Strivers display type sat alongside the Performance Club lockup. One brand on the wall, the same brand on the phone.
03 — Content Production

Built the digital programming with the coaches who run it on the floor.

I built the digital programming for the app alongside The St. James fitness instructors and sports coaches. Hired the video production team. Ran the onsite shoot at the facility. Owned the budget for production and post.

Programming with the instructors
Worked directly with the fitness instructors and sports coaches to translate floor-level training into structured digital programs.
Production team hired
Hired the video crew, scoped the shoot, and managed the onsite production at the St. James facility.
Budget and post owned
Owned production budget end to end. Production through post, including the edit, color, and final delivery to the app build.
Shot for the screen, not the gym
Framed every shot so the athlete sat at the center of a phone-sized rectangle. The camera worked for the mobile crop, not the brochure.
04 — App Store & Play Store Launch

Same brand, two store specs, one design language.

I designed the launch screenshots that put Strivers on the App Store and Google Play. The marketing system covered 100+ frames across iOS and Android with hero copy that did the work: "Maximize Your Potential," "Achieve more with expert trainers," "Follow your favorites, anytime from anywhere."

05 — Supporting Design

Strivers lived inside a bigger brand. I supported that brand too.

Alongside Strivers, I supported design on the main St. James digital surface. Membership signup as a responsive system across desktop, tablet, and mobile. The Sport 1.0 scheduling experience on tablet. The Performance Club wall graphic and floorplan at the physical facility. Same brand, same typography, same gold accent on the wall behind the front desk as on the app in the locker room.

Responsive membership signup

One brand, three breakpoints.

Membership 1.0 ran as a connected responsive system across desktop, tablet, and mobile. Black-on-cream hero with gold underline as the only accent. Editorial photography of training environments. Progressive disclosure so the page never asked for more than the screen could show.

Marketing surface aspirational. Form surface frictionless. Same system across every campaign: Military Offer, Valentine's Offer, member referrals, seasonal pushes.

How I worked

A small team in D.C., a dev team in Romania, and the bench I left behind.

I led design across internal and external teams, including an overseas dev team in Romania. Partnered directly with the CMO, the web developers, and the business strategists. Before I rolled off, I hired the UX designer and the digital product manager who took the work forward.
