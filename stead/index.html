The Challenge

Most productivity tools assume one kind of brain.

They reward streaks, punish gaps, and ask you to be consistent in exactly the way that doesn't fit how you actually work. Stead begins from the opposite premise: build for brains that need a different kind of start, first.

A planning companion that works whether you need momentum or predictability, holds the day without demanding you fill it, and never makes you feel behind. No streaks. No guilt. A standing arrangement with yourself.

01 — Brand & Visual System

Same house, different rooms.

One parchment paper across the whole product. Instrument Serif italic for moments of warmth, a clean sans for everything else. The screens don't feel like four products, they feel like four moments in one day. Differentiation runs on a single axis: the quality of light. Dawn on the dashboard, a vellum page for input, a lit lantern for active work, twilight when something is done. Every atmosphere holds 4.5:1 contrast against one warm ink.

One paper
Same parchment base across every screen. Atmosphere is layered over the paper as light, never replacing it. The grain texture is constant.
One type voice
Instrument Serif italic carries at least one warm sentence on every screen. A clean sans handles the rest.
A warm, calm palette
Dusty plum, sand, ember, rose. No pure black, no pure white. Accent shifts (plum to ember) signal state without changing the brand.
Accessibility by default
Every atmosphere shift stays under a few points of lightness and holds 4.5:1 contrast against the ink, tested. Calm is a feature, not a finish.
02 — Track A · Momentum Builder

When a thing won't start, we cut it small.

Name the big thing. Stead breaks it into small first moves, each tagged by the energy it asks for, low or high, so you can pick what fits the moment you're actually in.

There's no fail state. "Set this down, I'll come back" sits where a guilt trip would be. Finishing a move earns a soft, varied acknowledgment. Never a streak, never a count.

Big thing in, small moves out
A single honest input. Stead returns the first few steps, not a 40-item plan that becomes its own weight.
Energy-tagged steps
Each move is marked low or high energy so the next action matches capacity, not an idealized version of the day.
No fail state
"Set this down, I'll come back" replaces the missed-task guilt loop. Stepping away is a designed, named option.
Soft celebrations
Varied, quiet acknowledgments on completion. The screen itself becomes the reward, not a badge or a counter.
03 — Track B · Predictability Engine

The shape of your hours, with honest cushions between.

A visual timeline of the day. The cushions between blocks are on purpose, they're yours, and nothing needs to fill them. A heads-up arrives before each transition so a shift from deep work to a call never lands as a surprise.

04 — Onboarding · The Brain-Style Letter

A quiz that ends in a letter, not a score.

Onboarding is the first kindness. A short read returns one of three results: a Momentum Brain, a Predictability Brain, or a Blended Brain. Each lands as a personal letter that explains, warmly, how Stead will meet you, which track it leads with, and why you were never lazy or scattered.

Predictability Brain

A letter, not a leaderboard
The result is written prose in the product's voice. It reframes the user's brain as a fit to design for, not a flaw to fix.
05 — The System

Two tracks, one app, one house.

Underneath the screens is one system. A reusable paper primitive, a single radius logic, one ink. A copy-voice layer (friend, literary, plain) lets the same product speak the way each person needs. Built so the warmth scales, the contrast holds, and the whole thing stays one house no matter how many rooms it grows.

/* same house, different rooms — atmosphere shifts per screen,
   composed over one parchment base. no new colors, tested 4.5:1. */
.screen-dashboard {        /* dawn */
  background: linear-gradient(180deg, #F8E9C8 0%, #F5F0E8 45%);
}
.screen-active {           /* lantern */
  background: radial-gradient(ellipse 60% 55% at 50% 45%, #FBEFD8, #F0E6CD);
  animation: lantern-pulse 3s ease-in-out infinite;
}
.screen-complete {         /* twilight */
  background: linear-gradient(180deg, #E2DBE0 0%, #EFE9E0 70%);
}

Designed and built. Stead is a Next.js app: one design-token source of truth, a copy-voice layer, and an AI step-breakdown pipeline behind Track A. The atmospheres are real CSS, not mockups.

Composable paper system
One paper panel primitive with tone, elevation, and tilt. Every card, sheet, and surface composes from it. Built once, used everywhere.
A copy-voice engine
Friend, literary, or plain. The same flows re-voice themselves so the product can be tender, spare, or direct depending on the person.
One radius, one ink, one rule
5px on cards, full pill on actions, hairlines for separation. A single warm ink for all text. Never larger, never softer, never off-palette.
State as light, not chrome
The app signals where you are with the quality of light, not new colors or loud UI. Atmosphere does the work the badge usually does.
