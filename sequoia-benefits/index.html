Sequoia Benefits

Three person product team. One platform redesign, one mobile rebuild, in a regulated category.

Product Designer2019 to 202004 / 05

Use Case

Sequoia Benefits runs benefits administration for companies that cannot afford ambiguity. I was one of three people on the product team covering research, design and delivery.

We redesigned the platform and rebuilt the mobile experience on the same system. In a regulated, high trust category every state has to be legible, including the ones nobody wants to look at.
