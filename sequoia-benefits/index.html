The Challenge

Regulated software, due for a redesign and a rebuild at the same time.

Sequoia Benefits is a benefits administration platform for HR and people teams. When I joined, the platform was due for a redesign covering the core admin web experience and a full rebuild of the mobile application.

Regulated software where trust is the whole product. Every redesign decision had to defend itself against the user research and the compliance surface underneath it.

01 — Embedded

Three people. 2,500+ companies. No buffer.

Three people on the product team. 2,500+ companies on the other side of every screen we shipped. Worked alongside the VP of Product, PMs, and Product Marketing. Every UX and visual decision had to hold up against both client requirements and internal business needs.

Traveled with the marketing team to lead live feedback sessions across the country, translating observations into design decisions in real time, in front of stakeholders.

02 — Platform Redesign

UAM Super Admin, access groups, and the surface every workflow ran through.

Web product covering the Sequoia admin experience, including the User Access Management Super Admin surface and the workflows around creating and editing access groups, inviting and editing users, and managing benefits views.

User Access Management
The Super Admin surface where access groups, user permissions, and benefits views were created and managed. The control panel underneath every customer org.
Access Group workflows
Create, Edit, Add and Remove from Group. Group-level access logic that compounds across hundreds of users without breaking.
User lifecycle workflows
Invite, edit, remove. Standard at the surface, careful underneath. The platform sits between an employer and the benefits employees depend on.
Compliance as constraint
Every screen had to defend itself against the regulated environment underneath. Compliance didn't soften the design. It sharpened it.
03 — Mobile Rebuild

Full rebuild, not a refresh.

The Sequoia mobile application was rebuilt from the ground up. Different from a redesign. New architecture, new component set, new flows, designed alongside the platform redesign so they shipped as one product, not two.
