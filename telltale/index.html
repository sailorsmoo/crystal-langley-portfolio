Telltale Games

Eight years of game UI across five platforms, including the Game of the Year winning Walking Dead.

UI/UX Department Lead2011 to 201802 / 05

Use Case

Telltale Games shipped narrative games across web, mobile, PlayStation, Xbox and Wii U and more. I designed UI and UX there for eight years, and by the time Telltale closed I was UI/UX Department Lead, overseeing a team of three designers and two front end developers.

The work included The Walking Dead, which won Game of the Year, other popular IP work such as Game of Thrones, Batman, Stranger Things, Minecraft and Jurassic Park, and original and partner IP work including sleeper hit The Wolf Among Us, Poker Night and Tales from the Borderlands.

The Walking Dead

Based on Robert Kirkman's comic series. Season one follows Lee Everett, a convicted criminal given a shot at redemption when he takes in an orphaned girl named Clementine during the outbreak of a zombie apocalypse. Told across five episodes released through 2012, its choice-driven, character-first storytelling won numerous Game of the Year awards and set the template Telltale built the rest of the studio on. Led UI development, art direction and implementation.

Game of Thrones

Set during the War of the Five Kings, the game follows the fall and fight for survival of House Forrester, a noble family of the north loyal to the Starks. Players control five different Forrester family members across six episodes released in 2014 and 2015, with choices determining who lives long enough to hold the house together. Led UI development, art direction and implementation.

Batman: The Telltale Series

A choice-driven take on the Batman mythos set apart from any existing comic or film continuity. Players split time between Bruce Wayne and Batman as revelations about the Wayne family legacy threaten his reputation, while a militant new criminal group works to expose corruption in Gotham. Released in five episodes in 2016. Led UI development, art direction and implementation.

The Wolf Among UsTwo seasons

The Wolf Among Us

Based on Bill Willingham's Fables comics. Players take on Bigby Wolf, the reformed Big Bad Wolf now serving as sheriff of Fabletown, a secret community of fairy tale characters hiding out in New York City. A noir murder mystery told across five episodes from October 2013 to July 2014. Led UI development, art direction and implementation.

Tales from the Borderlands05 clips

Tales from the Borderlands

A spinoff of Gearbox's Borderlands series, set on the planet Pandora after the events of Borderlands 2. Hyperion company man Rhys and con artist Fiona are thrown together over a Vault key neither of them can walk away from. Known for its comedic tone, it ran five episodes from late 2014 into 2015. Led UI development, art direction and implementation.

Minecraft Story ModeYoungest audience

Minecraft Story Mode

Built with Mojang, the game invents its own hero, Jesse, and its own villain, the Wither Storm, found nowhere in the base game, for a quest to save the Minecraft world. Aimed at a younger audience than Telltale's other titles, it ran two seasons and thirteen episodes between 2015 and 2017. Led UI development, art direction and implementation.

Poker Night

Telltale's two poker-table crossovers, released in 2010 and 2013, seated players against a rotating cast of characters borrowed from other franchises, including Sam and Max, Team Fortress 2's Heavy, Borderlands' Claptrap, Ash from Evil Dead and Portal's GLaDOS as dealer, each one voiced and animated to talk trash in character. Led UI development, art direction and implementation.
